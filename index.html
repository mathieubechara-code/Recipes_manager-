Quick guide

          
How to use Meal Planner

        

        
      

      

        
1
Plan your week
Open Week → Plan week to suggest lunches, dinners, or both.

        
2
Keep meals you like
Tap 🔒 on a scheduled meal. Locked meals stay exactly as they are.

        
3
Change one suggestion
Tap ↻ beside a meal to replace only that lunch or dinner with another eligible recipe.

        
4
Regenerate the rest
Tap “Regenerate unlocked” to replace all scheduled meals in the visible week except the ones you locked.

        
5
Use Today for daily cooking
Open Today to see lunch and dinner, move to previous/next days, and open the full recipe.

      

      

        Example
        
You like Monday Salmon and Thursday Burrito → lock them 🔒 → tap Regenerate unlocked → Meal Planner changes the other meals only.
